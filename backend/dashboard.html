📚 Climate Learning Center

Understanding the terms behind the data helps you make informed decisions.

🌡️ Temperature

Measures how hot or cold the air is. Affects energy use, agriculture, and health.

💧 Humidity

The amount of water vapor in the air. High humidity makes it feel hotter and can cause discomfort.

💨 Wind Speed

How fast air is moving. Strong winds spread fires, affect flights, and generate energy.

🌧️ Rain Probability

Chance of precipitation. High probability (>80%) may cause flooding.

🔥 Fire Risk

Combination of high temperature, low humidity, and strong winds – increases wildfire danger.

🌊 Ocean Anomaly

Difference from normal sea surface temperature. Positive anomaly can cause coral bleaching.

⚡ Carbon Intensity

Amount of CO₂ emitted per unit of electricity (gCO₂/kWh). Lower is cleaner.

🌿 Pollen

Fine particles from plants that cause allergies. Higher levels affect respiratory health.

🏙️ UHI (Urban Heat Island)

Cities are warmer than rural areas due to concrete and human activity.

🛡️ Risk Score

Composite measure of climate vulnerability – higher score means lower risk.

⚠️ Alert Severity

Advisory – Caution; Watch – Be ready; Warning – Take action now.

🌍 Why This Matters

Understanding climate data helps communities prepare for disasters, reduce emissions, and build resilience.
