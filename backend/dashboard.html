📚 Climate Learning Center

Understanding the terms behind the data helps you make informed decisions.

🌡️ Temperature

Measures how hot or cold the air is. Affects energy, agriculture, and health.

💧 Humidity

Amount of water vapor in the air. High humidity makes it feel hotter.

💨 Wind Speed

How fast air is moving. Strong winds spread fires and generate energy.

🌧️ Rain Probability

Chance of precipitation. High probability (>80%) may cause flooding.

🔥 Fire Risk

High temperature + low humidity + strong winds = increased wildfire danger.

🌊 Ocean Anomaly

Difference from normal sea temperature. Positive anomaly = coral bleaching risk.

⚡ Carbon Intensity

CO₂ emitted per unit of electricity (gCO₂/kWh). Lower is cleaner.

🌿 Pollen

Fine plant particles that cause allergies. Higher levels affect respiratory health.

🏙️ UHI

Urban Heat Island – cities are warmer than rural areas due to concrete and activity.

🛡️ Risk Score

Composite climate vulnerability score – higher = lower risk.

⚠️ Alert Severity

Advisory = Caution; Watch = Be Ready; Warning = Take Action Now.
